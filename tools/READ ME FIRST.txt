GlowLyrics for Mac
==================

INSTALL
1. Drag GlowLyrics into the Applications folder.
2. Open GlowLyrics from Applications.

IF MACOS SAYS IT CAN'T BE OPENED
This app isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm once:
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to GlowLyrics.
  - Or, on older macOS versions: right-click (Control-click) the app > Open > Open.

USING IT
- A ♪ icon appears in the menu bar (top right). There's no Dock icon.
- Play a song in Spotify or Apple Music. When macOS asks whether GlowLyrics may control Spotify or Music, click OK. That's how it reads the song and playback position.
- Click the ♪ icon for settings, lyrics styles, full-screen Stage mode, and Quit.
- Drag the lyrics bar to move it. Scroll over it to resize the text. Double-click it for full screen.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free community lyrics database.
